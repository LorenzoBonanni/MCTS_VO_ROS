Why gamma differs per scene
---------------------------

The search holds obstacles frozen at their observed position (paper 4.2.1), so
it is only usable while they have not moved far. The effective horizon is
dt / (1 - gamma_step), and the condition is

    v_closing * horizon  <  r_robot + r_obstacle  =  0.15 + 0.10  =  0.25 m

Take the LONGEST horizon that still fits inside that margin. Faster obstacles
therefore want a shorter horizon, i.e. a smaller gamma. Measured collapse
points, as a fraction of the margin covered:

    intention_complex   fine to 71%, 6% goal at 99%, 0% beyond
    sinusoidal_complex  degrades from 39%
    intention           peaks at 60%, 0% at 181%
    sinusoidal          peaks at 95%, 40% at 181%


Note on range_metric
--------------------

get_discrete_space split its sample budget by sqrt(lo^2 + hi^2) instead of the
width hi - lo. "width" corrects it, "norm" reproduces every result to date.
Replaying 48357 recorded planner states, the action set differs in 24 of them
(0.05%), so no outcome campaign can resolve the two. Default is norm.
